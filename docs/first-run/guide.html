One resume for one job.

You talk. Your helper builds a private profile from work you already did, then writes one resume for one job.

You can chase more than one job. They all sit on one list. A short guide is ready the night before an interview.

You say when a sentence is wrong, and nothing is sent until you say so.

  2. 2

    Hand over old resumes or notes

    Your helper builds a private profile of your work. Later writing comes from that profile.

    Show this stepHide this step
    You do this
    Hand over old resumes, notes, or links to work you already published. Several are better than one.
    Why
    Your helper reads them and writes a private profile: the jobs you had, what you did, and what you did not do. Every resume and every night-before guide is written from that profile. Your own work builds that profile. The job ads tell your helper which jobs to write for.
    You get
    A profile you can correct. It stays on your computer. It is not a resume, and it is not published.

    You could say: "Here is my old resume, and a note about what that job really was."

  3. 3

    Answer one question at a time

    The profile matches what you would say out loud. Skip any question you do not know.

    Show this stepHide this step
    You do this
    Answer questions about jobs you have had, the kind of role you want, and where you can work. Correct a wrong line the moment you hear it. Say skip if you do not know.
    Why
    The papers are a start. The questions fill the gaps and take out anything you would not say. The profile is only useful if it sounds like you.
    You get
    A profile your helper can trust for the resumes and the night-before guide.

    Sample question: "What did you do at Contoso Labs?" Alex says: "I led one product launch. I did not manage a budget."

    Sample, made up. Alex Rivera at Contoso Labs.

  4. 4

    Bring the jobs you want

    You can bring more than one. Each resume is written for one job, after you say yes.

    Show this stepHide this step
    You do this
    Paste one link, or several. You can also say the company and the job title. Your helper checks that each link still opens.
    Why
    The list needs to know what you are chasing. A resume is written for one posting, so the sentences match that job. Your helper waits for a yes on each one. The other jobs sit on the list until you are ready.
    You get
    A set of real, open jobs. No resume is written until you say yes to that job.

    Alex says: "Yes, write one for Northwind. Leave Fabrikam on the list for now."

    Show this stepHide this step
    You do this
    Read each line as if you were saying it out loud.
    Why
    Your helper wrote it from your profile and from the one job you approved. A wrong line would go out in your name later. If a line is wrong, say so. Your helper takes it off.

    A resume for that one job. Nothing is emailed. Nothing is sent until you say so. Ask for the next job when you want another.

  8. 8

    Ask for a short guide the night before

    It is written from your profile, for that one interview, and it names what you have not done.

    Show this stepHide this step
    You do this
    Ask your helper for a short guide the night before that conversation.
    Why
    The guide is built from your profile and from that one job, so you can talk about work you really did. It also names what you have not done, so you do not have to pretend.
    You get
    A short page for that interview. It is not a script for a job you have not done.

    You could say: "Write the short guide for tomorrow."
