Questions people ask first

Plain answers. No tech knowledge needed.

What is an AI agent?

It's an AI helper, like Claude, that can do tasks on your computer — not just chat. It can download RB, start it, read your files, and fill in forms. It should ask you before doing anything big.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Your agent handles the technical parts. You read, click, drag files, and answer questions in plain words.

Does this cost money?

RB itself is free. It uses your existing AI plan (about $20 a month for Claude or similar). Heavy use in one day may hit your plan's limit. If that happens, wait and try again later — your work is saved.

Where do my files go?

Two folders, side by side on your computer. You put your files in resume-builder / my-documents. RB saves the new resumes it writes in resume-builder / output. You can open both folders any time.

Is my information private?

RB runs on your computer. This page is not on the public internet. Your AI agent does read your files to help you, so your AI provider's privacy rules apply. Don't put things like your Social Security number in the folder.

Will it apply to jobs for me?

No. RB writes resumes and tracks jobs. You decide what to send and you send it yourself.

Can I stop? Can I undo it?

Yes. Close this tab to pause. Tell your agent "Stop the resume-builder server" to shut it off. To remove it, delete the resume-builder folder (save my-documents first if you want your files).

How do I get back here next time?

Tell your agent: "Start resume-builder and open the home page." Your files and answers will still be there.
