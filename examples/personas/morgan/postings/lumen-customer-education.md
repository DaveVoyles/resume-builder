# Customer education specialist

Company: Lumen Cloud (fictional)
Location: Columbus, OH (hybrid)

Lumen Cloud is hiring a customer education specialist to teach customers how to use its software.

You will:
- Run live training workshops for new customers.
- Write help guides and handouts.
- Collect feedback after each session and improve the material.

You have:
- Experience teaching or training adults or students.
- Clear writing and an interest in technical documentation.
